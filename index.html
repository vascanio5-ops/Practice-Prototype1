Grammar and vocabulary 8.1
ZERO AND FIRST CONDITIONAL ▶

Use the zero conditional to talk about general truths and facts.

If/When + present simple, present simple

If you recycle, your rubbish doesn't go into a landfill.

When we throw things away, where do they go?

Use the first conditional to talk about real possibilities in the future.

If + present simple, will/won't + infinitive

If we don't act on climate change, we won't have a future.

If we destroy rainforests, where will the animals go?
